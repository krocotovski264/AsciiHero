  via Space Station 14 (github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14, Resources/Audio/Effects/Footsteps):
  step_water      water1-4.ogg (footsteps in water)

All the loops were trimmed, made seamless (cross-faded end into start), mixed to mono and levelled.

Other assets of AsciiHero (not sounds):
  Resources/rathead.bytes  the rat-man's head, cut from "rat" of the 3moji set, CC BY-SA 4.0 (modified: head only, rescaled, recoloured)
  Resources/cuts.bytes     the story's scene clips, from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database (mocap.cs.cmu.edu), free to use;
                           subjects 114, 26, 111, 18, retargeted to the game's skeleton
  Resources/cuts.bytes     also "die" (the fall backwards): CMU subject 90, clip 90_18 (RugPullFall)
  Resources/kaykit.bytes   KayKit Character Packs (Skeletons 1.0, Adventurers 1.0) by Kay Lousberg, CC0 1.0
                           (www.kaylousberg.com): Skeleton_Rogue_Hood, Skeleton_Warrior_Helmet, Barbarian_Cape,
                           Skeleton_Warrior_Head and _Jaw - single pieces, rescaled, recoloured by their palette
  Resources/kaykit.bytes   also "yskull"/"yjaw": the BodyParts3D skull (DBCLS, CC BY 4.0) of the cave set, its lower jaw
                           cut off to move on its own (Yorick in the pocket, the risen, the fallen knights)
  Creatures: their speeds, habits and kinds of attack follow D&D 5e monsters (wererat, zombie: SRD 5.1 under
  CC BY 4.0; ratfolk: Tome of Beasts, Kobold Press) - in spirit, not in numbers
